Overview

What is BIS Sahayak?

BIS Sahayak is an AI-powered assistant designed to simplify access to information related to Indian Standards and BIS services. It allows users to ask questions in natural language and receive clear, relevant information.

Natural language search across 21,000+ Indian Standards
Direct clause citations and mandatory compliance references
Structured tables for chemical & mechanical property limits
Simple 3-Step Process

How It Works

01 — Ask

Ask

Ask your question in natural language regarding any product standard, testing clause, certification scheme, or hallmarking rule.

02 — Understand

Understand

BIS Sahayak processes the query and identifies relevant Indian Standards, Quality Control Orders (QCOs), and regulatory guidelines.

03 — Get Information

Get Information

Receive a clear answer along with relevant references, clause specifications, and parameter comparison tables where available.
